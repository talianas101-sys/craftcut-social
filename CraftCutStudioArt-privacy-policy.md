# Privacy Policy: CraftCut Social Poster

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

CraftCut Social Poster is a private tool used only by **CraftCut Studio Art** (an Etsy shop, [etsy.com/shop/CraftCutStudioArt](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CraftCutStudioArt)) to schedule and publish marketing posts about its own products on its own Pinterest and TikTok accounts.

## What the tool does
- Publishes pins with images, titles, descriptions and links to our own Etsy listings on **our own** Pinterest business account.
- Reads the list of boards on **our own** Pinterest account so posts go to the right board.
- Sends videos as drafts to **our own** TikTok account's inbox, where we review and publish them by hand.

## What data it uses
- Only data from the CraftCut Studio Art accounts that we sign in with ourselves: board names and IDs, the IDs of pins and posts we create, and the sign-in tokens Pinterest and TikTok issue to us.
- This data is kept in our private content calendar (a Google Sheet) and in our private automation account (n8n), and is used only to run our own posting schedule.

## What it does not do
- It does not let anyone else sign in, and it does not access other people's accounts, pins, boards, videos or personal information.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
- It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

## Removing access
We can disconnect the tool at any time from our Pinterest and TikTok account settings. When we do, the sign-in tokens stop working and we delete them from our records.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: send us a message through our Etsy shop, [etsy.com/shop/CraftCutStudioArt](https://www.etsy.com/shop/CraftCutStudioArt).
